This is the entire layout of this plain collectable game; its a simple blank background with the collectable item sprinkled throughout it. The character starts up in the top left, and can move around the layout. He cannot leave the edges of the layout, nor die. The "text" in the top left remains stationary, a score counter adding 100 points per candy collected.
This is a ongoing top down shooter game. The objective is relatively simple, little krill guy in a bubble shooting at the big whale sharks coming at you. Technically speaking the game doesn't have an end, it just continues endlessly until you, well, die. Each whale shark shot is five points added to your overall score, but it takes five hits total for them to die.
